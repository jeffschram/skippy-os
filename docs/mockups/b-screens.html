I keep thinking about turning half the garage into a gym. A rack, a bench, rubber flooring. Not sure it fits with the car in there.

A half rack needs about 8 by 6 feet, plus room on each side to load the bar. So the first thing to find out is the clear width of the bay with the car parked. Check the ceiling height for overhead presses too, and whether the floor slopes toward the door.
